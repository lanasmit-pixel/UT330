About the Assignment

For this assignment, my group worked together as a product design team to develop a multi-screen user experience in Figma. We were responsible for creating a set of screens that worked together as one complete task flow. The assignment focused on interaction design, making intentional design choices, and thinking about how someone would move through the experience from beginning to end.

We worked through the project in multiple rounds, discussing our ideas, reviewing our design choices, and thinking about how we could improve the experience. The goal was not just to make the screens look good, but to make sure the information, layout, and order of the screens made sense for the user.

My Role

My main responsibility was designing the third frame of our project. I focused on my assigned screen while keeping the other screens in mind so that the overall experience would feel consistent. I had to think about what information belonged on the screen, how it should be organized, and how it connected to the steps before and after it.

Even though I was responsible for one frame, I was still part of the larger group process. We discussed our work together, shared ideas, and helped each other work through decisions. This made it easier to understand how each person's contribution affected the final design.

Working as a Team

One of the things that went well was how well our group worked together. We communicated, listened to each other's ideas, and worked through the assignment collaboratively. Everyone had a part to contribute, but we were also willing to help each other when we needed to make decisions or figure something out.

I liked that we could divide up the work while still making decisions as a group. It showed me that designing a product is not just about completing an individual screen. The screens have to fit together, and everyone needs to understand the overall goal. Collaboration helped us create a more connected experience than if we had each worked separately.

What I Learned

This assignment helped me better understand how interaction design works in a team setting. I learned that each screen needs to serve its own purpose while also supporting the full user journey. Consistency, communication, and considering the user's needs are all important when bringing individual pieces of a design together.

I also saw how feedback and group discussion can help a design improve over multiple rounds. Instead of treating the first version as the final answer, we could look at what was working, discuss what could be clearer, and make changes together. Overall, I enjoyed working with my group and seeing how our different contributions came together to create one project.
