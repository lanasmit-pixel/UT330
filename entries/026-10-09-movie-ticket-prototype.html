Designing an Online Movie Ticket Purchasing Experience

October 9, 2026 · Figma Prototyping Lab

For this assignment, I created a low-fidelity Figma prototype for an online movie ticket purchasing experience. The goal was to map out one complete path a user could take, from selecting a movie to reaching a confirmation screen after choosing a seat and entering payment information.

I designed four screens to show how the experience progresses. The first screen allows the user to select a date and find a movie. The second screen focuses on the seating map, where the user can review their selected seat and confirm their choice. The third screen shows the checkout process, including payment information, and the final screen displays the ticket confirmation.

Designing the interaction

One thing I focused on was making sure each screen connected logically to the next. I wanted someone who had never seen the design before to be able to understand what to do and where to go next. I also worked on keeping the layout consistent across the screens so the experience felt connected instead of like four separate designs.

What I learned

This assignment helped me think more carefully about the steps people take when using a digital product. Even something as familiar as buying a movie ticket involves several decisions and interactions. The way information is organized, the labels on buttons, and the feedback a user receives can all affect how easy the process feels.

I also learned more about how a prototype can communicate the flow of an experience before a fully developed product exists. Rather than focusing on making everything look finished, I had to think about the structure, sequence, and purpose of each screen.
