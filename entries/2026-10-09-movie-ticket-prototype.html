Designing an Online Movie Ticket Purchasing Experience

Overview

For this project, I created a low-fidelity prototype in Figma for an online movie ticket purchasing experience. The goal was to design a simple process that allows a user to choose a movie, select seats, complete checkout, and receive confirmation of their purchase.

My Design

I created four screens to represent one complete user journey. Each screen focuses on a different step in the ticket purchasing process.

  • 01. Showtimes: The user chooses a movie and selects a showtime.
  • 02. Seat Selection: The user chooses available seats using a seat map.
  • 03. Checkout: The user reviews the order and enters payment information.
  • 04. Confirmation: The user receives confirmation after completing the purchase.

What I Learned

This project helped me think about how users move through a digital experience and how each screen connects to the next. I learned that the design needs to provide clear information at every step so users understand what to do and can complete their task without confusion.

I also practiced organizing frames in Figma, creating reusable components, using Auto Layout, and connecting screens to represent a complete user flow. Working through the process helped me understand how interaction design involves both the appearance of a screen and the experience of moving through it.
